# Albuquerque--Majid 1999 --- octonions as a twisted group algebra

## Why this source matters

Supplies the framework in which the S/S' difference becomes a single object: the sign function chi on F_2^4 x F_2^4.

## Claims taken from it

- CD algebras as **twisted group algebras of F_2^n** with a sign function.
- In this language S and S' differ by **chi = -1 on pairs of distinct nonzero points of the founding F_2^3, +1 elsewhere**.
- **chi is not a 2-cocycle** (Prop. 7.3) --- so the difference is not a coboundary-style relabelling, and indeed no signed relabelling of the sixteen basis units carries S to S' (Prop. 7.4, exhaustive over GL(4,2)).

## Caveats

The 'orientation bit' framing is the paper's, not Albuquerque--Majid's. The non-cocycle property is what makes it a genuine obstruction rather than a choice of basis.
