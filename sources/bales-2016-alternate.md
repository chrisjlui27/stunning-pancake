# Bales 2016 --- an alternate Cayley--Dickson product

## Why this source matters

The title is one word away from the mirror. Worth settling quickly so it can be set aside.

## Claims taken from it (abstract only)

- Uses 'a doubling product for the algebras **different from yet equivalent to** the ones commonly used' together with a **shuffle basis** numbering, producing a highly periodic twist and a closed-form formula for the product of any two basis vectors.
- For a product P, P(e_p, e_q) = P^T(e_q, e_p); the basis multiplication table of P is the transpose of that of P^T.

## Risk to the paper

**Low.** 'Different from yet **equivalent to**' is the operative phrase: equivalent means isomorphic, so no new algebra. This looks like a change of presentation (shuffle basis, periodic twist), not a new object. The mirror double is not equivalent to CD --- that is the whole point, and it is proved four independent ways (Cor. 7.5).
