# Bales 2016 --- the eight doubling products

## Why this source matters

The taxonomy the whole mirror construction sits inside. The mirror product (2) **is** Bales's P1^T; without this source the mirror double looks arbitrary rather than one of a classified eight.

## Claims taken from it

- **There are eight doubling products yielding a Cayley--Dickson algebra when applied at every level starting from R.** The four "transposed" products give the opposite algebras of the other four.
- **Bales considers only uniform towers** --- one product used at every level. The uniform P1^T tower is a *different* algebra from S': its dimension-8 stage is already M(H) rather than O, and its dimension-16 stage has a 6-dimensional derivation algebra (Remark 3.8).

## Caveats

The uniform-tower restriction is the whole opening: applying one of the eight products *once*, on top of standard O, is the question Bales does not ask. Confirm the numbering convention P0..P3/P0^T..P3^T matches before citing specific products --- the mirror paper's Theorem 3.6 assigns S to {P0, P3, P0^T, P3^T} and S' to {P1, P2, P1^T, P2^T}.
