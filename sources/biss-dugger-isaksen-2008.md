# BDI 2008 --- large annihilators in CD algebras

## Why this source matters

Source of the S-side zero-divisor manifold, and the natural target for Open Question 3 (do the large-annihilator results have mirror analogues?).

## Claims taken from it

- **Z(S) ~= V2(R^7)**, an 11-manifold (Thm. 1.3).
- **All annihilators in S are exactly 4-dimensional** (Prop. 12.1 gives exactness; Moreno gives the mod-4 congruence).

## Caveats

Open Question 3 asks whether dim Ann == 0 (mod 2) rather than (mod 4) is the general mirror pattern. That is a conjecture, not a consequence of anything in this source.
