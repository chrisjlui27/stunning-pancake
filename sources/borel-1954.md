# Borel 1954 --- cohomology of compact connected Lie groups

## Why this source matters

Supplies H^*(G2; F2), which is what proves P(S') is *not* homotopy equivalent to P(S).

## Claims taken from it

- **H^*(G2; F2) = F2[x3]/(x3^4) tensor Lambda(x5)**, so the cup square H^3 -> H^6 is **nonzero**.
- Against this: H^*(V2(R^7) x S^3; F2) = Lambda(x5, x6) tensor Lambda(y3) with **y3^2 = 0**. Both H^3 are 1-dimensional, so the rings are not isomorphic. Hence P(S') is not homotopy equivalent to G2 ~= P(S).

## Caveats

Cited jointly with Mimura--Toda [17]. This is the cleanest *topological* separation of the two pair manifolds and does not depend on any of the algebra conventions.
