# Brown 1967 --- Aut of generalized CD algebras

## Why this source matters

The S-side automorphism group, and the specific automorphism that fails to survive the mirror.

## Claims taken from it

- **Aut(S) = G2 x S^3**.
- Contains an **order-three automorphism** which **does not survive** in S': Aut(S') = G2 x Z/2 (Thm. 7.1). Verified computationally (check7, check9).

## Caveats

The G2 x S^3 vs G2 x Z/2 contrast is one of four independent reasons S and S' are non-isomorphic (Cor. 7.5) --- the others are dim Z (11 vs 13), generic dim Ann (4 vs 2), and the structure of the alternative elements.
