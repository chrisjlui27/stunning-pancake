# Cawagas 2004 --- structure and zero divisors of S

## Why this source matters

Source of the quasi-octonion algebra Otilde, which Theorem 3.3 identifies with M(H) --- the smaller model of the whole story.

## Claims taken from it

- Introduces the **quasi-octonion algebra Otilde** = H + H(eps*l) inside S.
- Corollary 3.4: **M(H) is isomorphic to Otilde**, for any quaternion subalgebra H subset O and unit eps perp H.
- Also cited for the **finite geometry of the multiplication table**.

## Caveats

Loop-theoretic vocabulary throughout; translating 'subloop' statements into subalgebra statements needs care.
