# Chan--Dokovic 2006 --- subalgebras of the real sedenions

## Why this source matters

Independent identification of M(H): their algebra S8. Corroborates Cawagas from a different direction.

## Claims taken from it

- Their **S8** (Thm. 8.1) is the **unique conjugacy class of 8-dimensional subalgebras of S with zero divisors**, and equals M(H) = H + H(l*e).
- Notation differs: H + H(l*e) here vs H + H(eps*l) in Cor. 3.4.

## Caveats

Two independent sources ([8] and [10]) reaching the same 8-dimensional algebra by different routes is good evidence the identification is right. The *ordering* convention in H(l*e) vs H(eps*l) is exactly the kind of thing the mirror construction is sensitive to --- check it rather than assuming.
