# de Marrais 2000 --- assessors and box-kites

## Why this source matters

Source of the 84-count that the mirror's 112-count is measured against.

## Claims taken from it

- **42 'assessors'** / **84 two-term zero divisors** in S --- the pairs e_i +/- e_j with j != i+8.
- Against this, S' has **112** two-term basis zero divisors: for 1 <= i <= 7 and 8 <= j <= 15, *both* e_i + e_j and e_i - e_j are zero divisors (56 index pairs).
- **Both algebras have exactly 336 ordered pairs** of two-term elements with product zero. Machine-verified (check9).

## Caveats

Idiosyncratic terminology ('box-kites', 'assessors') and a non-refereed preprint. The counts themselves are finite checks and are independently verified in the accompanying code, so the citation is for attribution rather than authority.
