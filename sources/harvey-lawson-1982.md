# Harvey--Lawson 1982 --- calibrated geometries

## Why this source matters

Standard reference for the G2 facts the paper uses freely in Sec. 2.3.

## Claims taken from it

- G2 acts **transitively on S^6** with stabilizer SU(3), **transitively on V2(R^7)** with stabilizer SU(2), and **transitively on quaternion subalgebras** Gr_H(O) ~= G2/SO(4).
- The stabilizer of an orthonormal pair (u, n) **fixes H(u, n) pointwise**.

## Caveats

Cited jointly with Salamon--Walpuski [20]; either suffices for these facts.
