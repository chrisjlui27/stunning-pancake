# JS working notes — own prior work

## Why this source matters

Reference [16] of the mirror-sedenions paper is **your own working notes**, and
the paper leans on them for two things that are not proved in it:

1. **The colour vocabulary** used in Prop. 5.4 — e₁,…,e₇ are *hues* and e_{i+8}
   is the *ultraviolet* of e_i. The statement "in S′ a hue with its own
   ultraviolet, or with the bare ultraviolet ℓ, is not innocent, and it is killed
   by every other hue minus that hue's ultraviolet" is stated in this vocabulary
   and is only meaningful with the notes in hand.
2. **The numerical census** behind Open Question 2 — "over all sign functions on
   F₂⁴ with quaternionic lines indicates that S and S′ are the only two with
   eight octaves and 14-dimensional derivation algebra."

## Claims taken from it

- The receipt-calculus / colour vocabulary (hues, ultraviolets) for indexing
  sedenion basis elements.
- A **numerical census over sign functions on F₂⁴ with quaternionic lines**,
  finding S and S′ to be the only two with eight octaves and dim Der = 14.
  The paper explicitly flags that **a proof is wanted** (Open Question 2).

## Caveats

**These notes are not in this vault.** JS-1–41 and JS-LANDSCAPE-II exist
somewhere else — chat history, Cowork, Drive, local disk. That makes them a
single point of failure for a published paper: a referee asking what the colour
vocabulary means, or asking to see the census, cannot be answered from anything
committed here.

This is the clearest concrete case for the vault existing at all.
