# Moreno 1998 --- zero divisors of CD algebras

## Why this source matters

The result the paper reinterprets. Moreno's G2-torsor theorem is the benchmark that S' fails to satisfy while having the same G2 --- which is the paper's sharpest rhetorical move.

## Claims taken from it

- **P(S) is isomorphic to G2** (Cor. 2.14) --- the pair manifold of the sedenions is a G2-torsor, the principal SU(2)-bundle G2 -> G2/SU(2) = V2(R^7).
- **dim Ann(x) == 0 mod 4** for sedenion zero divisors (Cor. 1.17).
- Moreno's proof realizes a zero-divisor pair as a **Cayley frame**.

## Caveats

The mirror paper's reading --- that the torsor property encodes alignment of the founding octave's orientation with the doubling twist, not G2 alone --- is *the paper's interpretation of Moreno*, not a claim Moreno makes. Attribute it carefully.
