# Reggiani 2024 --- Riemannian geometry of sedenion zero divisors

## Why this source matters

The direct model for Open Question 1. Reggiani does for P(S) exactly what the paper wants done for P(S').

## Claims taken from it

- **P(S) with the induced metric is isometric to G2** with a naturally reductive left-invariant metric.
- It **fibres over G2/SO(4) with fibres locally isometric to S^3 x S^3**.
- Reggiani studies **Einstein deformations** of this metric --- the template for asking whether P(S') is Einstein for some rescaling.

## Caveats

The fibre comparison is delicate and worth stating precisely: the SO(4) fibre of P(S) and the SO(3) x S^3 fibre of P(S') over the *same* Grassmannian are two different Z/2-quotients of S^3 x S^3. That coincidence-that-isn't is easy to misreport.
