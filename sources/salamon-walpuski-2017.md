# Salamon--Walpuski 2017 --- notes on the octonions

## Why this source matters

Alternative standard reference for the same G2 transitivity facts; likely the more readable entry point.

## Claims taken from it

- Same G2 facts as [14]: transitivity on S^6, on V2(R^7), and on quaternion subalgebras, with the stated stabilizers.

## Caveats

Expository notes rather than a research paper --- good for checking conventions, which is where octonion arguments usually go wrong.
