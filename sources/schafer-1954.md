# Schafer 1954 --- derivations of CD algebras

## Why this source matters

Establishes Der = g2 for CD algebras past O --- the invariant that does *not* distinguish S from S'.

## Claims taken from it

- **Der(S) = g2** (dimension 14). The mirror paper verifies **Der(S') = g2** as well, so the derivation algebra is blind to the mirror.
- Machine-verified: dim Der = 14 for S, S', and all thirty-two Bales candidates; **6** for M(H), the uniform P1^T tower, and M(M(H)).

## Caveats

That Der cannot tell the algebras apart is why the paper needs the zero-divisor geometry and Aut to do the separating work.
