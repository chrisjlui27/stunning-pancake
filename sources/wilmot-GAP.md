# Wilmot — open gap in the literature search

## Why this source matters

Named in conversation as one of three good starting points for hypercomplex
algebra research, alongside Reggiani and Moreno. Reggiani and Moreno are
references [19] and [18] of the mirror-sedenions paper. **Wilmot is not cited at
all.**

That asymmetry is the single most exposed point in the paper right now. §1 claims:

> "We found no prior description of the zero divisors, annihilators, spectrum,
> automorphisms, or subalgebra geometry of S′, and no prior formulation of the
> mirror double as a construction."

Any prior treatment of alternative sedenion doubling products would land exactly
there.
